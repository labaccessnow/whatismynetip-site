❓ Frequently asked questions

What is my IP address?

Your IP (Internet Protocol) address is the unique number your network uses to communicate on the internet. This page shows your public IP — the address the internet sees, assigned by your ISP.

What's the difference between a public and private IP address?

Your public IP is your router's internet-facing address, shared by every device on your network through NAT. Private IPs such as 192.168.x.x are used only inside your local network and aren't reachable from the internet.

Why does my IP address keep changing?

Most ISPs assign dynamic IP addresses that change periodically. To keep a stable name for a changing IP, use Dynamic DNS (DDNS), which maps a hostname that updates automatically.

What is Dynamic DNS (DDNS)?

Dynamic DNS gives your ever-changing IP a permanent hostname that updates automatically whenever your IP changes — so you can always reach a home server, camera or NAS by name. Claim one above.

What is an ASN?

An Autonomous System Number identifies the network operator — usually your ISP — that owns your IP range. For example, AS701 belongs to Verizon.

How do I check if a port is open?

Use the port checker, which connects back to your public IP from our server to confirm whether a TCP port — for a game server or self-hosted app — is reachable from the internet.

Can my IP address identify me?

Your IP reveals your approximate location (city level) and your ISP, but not your name or exact address. City-level IP geolocation is often off by tens of kilometres.

How do I hide my IP address?

A VPN or proxy routes your traffic through another server, so websites see that server's IP instead of yours. The VPN/datacenter check on this page flags whether an IP looks like a VPN or hosting provider.
